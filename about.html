How It Works

Understanding our secure voting process

Voter Registration

Administrators register eligible voters by capturing their biometric data using advanced face recognition technology, ensuring secure identity verification.

Face Detection

Using MediaPipe and OpenCV, our system captures and processes facial landmarks to create unique biometric signatures for each registered voter.

Authentication

During voting, the system compares real-time facial data with stored biometric information to verify voter identity before allowing ballot access.

Secure Voting

Once authenticated, voters can securely cast their ballots. The system ensures one vote per person and maintains ballot privacy throughout the process.

Real-time Results

Vote tallies are updated instantly and displayed through interactive charts, providing transparent and immediate election results.

Audit Trail

Complete transaction logs and audit trails ensure accountability and enable post-election verification while maintaining voter anonymity.
